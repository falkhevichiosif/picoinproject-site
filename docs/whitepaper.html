PICoin White Paper

Version 1.0

1. Introduction

The PICoin Project is designed to provide participants with indirect exposure to a diversified portfolio of global assets through the PICoin token.

The project's long-term objective is to establish an investment fund whose capitalization may increase through income generated by securities, market indices, and commodities (such as gold and industrial metals). The fund is intended to support the long-term development of the ecosystem through the acquisition and management of productive assets.

The target audience includes private investors, cryptocurrency enthusiasts, and individuals interested in long-term capital accumulation and protection against inflation.

2. Problem

Traditional pension systems and bank deposits often provide limited returns and may not adequately protect savings from inflation.

Capital markets and investment opportunities are frequently inaccessible to smaller investors due to financial, geographic, or institutional barriers.

Many existing cryptocurrency projects are primarily speculative in nature or lack a clear connection to productive real-world assets.

3. Solution

The PICoin Project provides an opportunity to participate in a global asset-oriented ecosystem through the PICoin token.

The long-term value of PICoin is intended to be influenced by the growth and development of the fund, the value of its underlying assets, and market demand for the token.

Participants may acquire, hold, and trade PIC tokens through supported platforms and decentralized markets. The market value of the token is determined by supply and demand and is not guaranteed by the project.

Blockchain technology is used to promote transparency, verifiability, and accessibility within the ecosystem.

4. Economic Model

Total Supply: 750,000,000,000 (750 billion) PIC.

The initial distribution of tokens is carried out through the Stellar infrastructure and includes the following categories:

  • 300 billion PIC — Stellar ICO — formation of the fund’s charter capital;
  • 300 billion PIC — Stellar Treasury — phased sale and fund development;
  • 75 billion PIC — Stellar MyTeam — team and project development;
  • 10 billion PIC — Stellar Volunteers — support for volunteer activities;
  • 10 billion PIC — Stellar Org — organizational and infrastructure needs;
  • 55 billion PIC — Stellar Reserve — project reserve and ecosystem sustainability.

Token issuance is carried out through the official Stellar Issuer of the project.

Income and Capitalization

  • The primary growth model of the project is based on increasing the fund’s capitalization and the long-term value of the PIC token.
  • Fund income is primarily directed toward reinvestment and asset expansion.
  • Ownership of PIC tokens does not guarantee income or dividends.

Token Burning

  • Token burning is possible only through approved procedures and within the framework of the project’s long-term strategy.
  • Burning may be used for structural changes and transitions to future stages of ecosystem development.

Token Value Growth

  • The value of PIC may be influenced by the total value of the fund’s assets, reinvestment strategy, market conditions, and participant demand.
  • The market price of PIC is determined by supply and demand and is not guaranteed by the project.

The current structure of Stellar wallets and technical information are published in the official project documentation and stellar.toml.

5. Technical Architecture

PIC tokens are issued and managed through the Stellar network, providing transparent and publicly verifiable transaction records.

Project documentation and selected public data may be stored using decentralized storage technologies such as IPFS, Storj, and Lighthouse.

Future development may include the integration of oracle services for obtaining external asset and market data.

The technical architecture of the project is designed to support transparency, accessibility, and long-term ecosystem development.

6. Participation Mechanism

Participants may acquire PIC tokens through project distribution channels, supported exchanges, or decentralized markets.

The long-term value of the PIC token may be influenced by the growth of the fund, the value of its underlying assets, market conditions, and participant demand.

Participants may hold, transfer, or trade PIC tokens. The market price of PIC is determined by supply and demand and is not guaranteed by the project.

Transaction records are publicly verifiable through the Stellar network, supporting transparency and accessibility within the ecosystem.

10. Conclusion

The PICoin Project aims to provide ordinary people with an opportunity to participate in global investment assets through the PICoin token.

A transparent distribution model, a long-term development approach, and a focus on productive assets are intended to support the sustainable growth of the ecosystem.
