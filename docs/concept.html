Project Concept

PICoinproject combines blockchain infrastructure with long-term capital accumulation principles.

  • PIC is used as a participation unit within the system.
  • Capital is directed into the Project structure and asset accumulation model.
  • The architecture is designed for gradual long-term growth.
  • Transparency and separation of flows are part of the system design.

The Project is built on Stellar infrastructure and designed with scalability and accessibility in mind.
