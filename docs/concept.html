Project Concept

The PICoin Project combines blockchain infrastructure with the principles of long-term capital accumulation.

  • PICoin serves as the participation unit within the ecosystem.
  • Capital is directed into the project's asset accumulation model.
  • The architecture is designed for gradual long-term growth.
  • Transparency and the separation of operational flows are integral to the system design.

The project is built on Stellar infrastructure and designed with scalability and accessibility in mind.
